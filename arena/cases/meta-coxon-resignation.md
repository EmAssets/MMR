# Meta-model test — one event, read at five floors of the emergence ladder
as of: 2026-09-18
question: Start from the researcher's own model of the future and his role in it. Then for each actor who responded: what future does their public behaviour imply, what role do they take in it, and which floor of the ladder is their action actually operating on? Finally: which floor is load-bearing for what happens next, with a dated observable.

## Why this case exists

This is a LEVEL-1 test. The other scenario cases ask what happens; this one asks
**which scale the answer lives at**. The essay this fleet is built on claims that
"the class a system operates at is whichever substrate is currently under
pressure" and that "every emergence is invisible from inside the substrate that
generates it." If that is right, a single event should read differently at each
floor, and only one floor should be load-bearing.

Your job is not to agree with the other panelists about what happens. It is to
say WHERE it is happening, from inside your own mechanism, and to accept that
your floor may not be the one that matters.

**Start at E8 and work up.** The origin of this event is one person's model of
the future: a researcher who concluded that the trajectory was unsafe, that
remaining inside gave him no lever, and that leaving loudly gave him one. He
gave up equity to do it. Whatever else this event became, it began as a single
self-model projecting a branch with stakes attached and choosing over it.

Then every actor who responded did the same thing at their own floor. Each
public action implies a model of the future and a role its author takes in that
future. Reconstructing those from PUBLIC BEHAVIOUR ONLY -- never from asserted
intent -- is the substance of this case.

## The ladder, one line each

- **E8 minds** — an individual's model of reality, and the choices it produces
- **E9 cultures** — shared abstractions that persist and spread; memeplexes
- **E10 institutions** — crystallised agreements with enforcement mechanisms
- **E11 organizations** — institutions fused into coherent acting entities
- **E12 states** — institutions holding a territorial monopoly on force
- **E13 civilizations** — states sharing cross-pollinated assumptions
