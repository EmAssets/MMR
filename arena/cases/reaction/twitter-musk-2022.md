# Musk completes the Twitter acquisition
as of: 2022-10-27

## Chronology — dated, sourced facts only

- **2022-10-27** — Elon Musk completes the acquisition of Twitter and dismisses
  CEO Parag Agrawal, CFO Ned Segal and policy head Vijaya Gadde.
- **2022-10-28** — Musk posts "the bird is freed," referring to his own takeover.
- **2022-10-28** — Thierry Breton, EU internal market commissioner, replies
  directly to Musk's post: "In Europe, the bird will fly by our rules."
- **2022-11-04** — Roughly half of Twitter's staff are laid off by email.
- **2022-11-04 onward** — Major advertisers including General Motors pause
  spending, citing the layoffs and uncertainty about content moderation.
- **2022-11-09** — Musk launches paid verification; impersonation accounts
  proliferate, including a fake Eli Lilly account announcing free insulin.
- **2022-11-11** — Eli Lilly's share price falls and the company issues a
  statement apologising, responding to the fake account.
- **2022-11-11** — Paid verification is suspended, responding to the
  impersonation wave.
- **after** — Commentary and press analysis of the advertiser pause as evidence
  that advertiser dependence, not user sentiment, constrained the platform.
