# Coxon resignation — reaction layers
as of: 2026-09-18

## Chronology — dated, sourced facts only

- **2026-09-09** — Jacob Coxon, an Anthropic safety researcher, resigns and posts
  publicly: "I resigned from Anthropic today... Neither company is acting
  responsibly. They are racing straight to self-improving superintelligence and
  gambling with our lives." He forfeited equity to leave.
- **within 24 hours** — the post reaches ~172M views, ~800k likes and ~200k
  reposts. A mass online audience amplifies the resignation post.
- **shortly after** — An Anthropic alignment science lead publicly endorses the
  resignation post: "Jacob here is correct... I personally think it's greater
  than 10% chance within the next decade."
- **shortly after** — A second Anthropic alignment team lead states publicly that
  Coxon was a senior researcher they had pitched to stay, responding to his
  departure.
- **2026-09-12** — Dario Amodei publishes "We Must Pace the Frontier", proposing
  embedded third-party evaluators such as METR with employee-like access to
  verify safety commitments. It follows the resignation and the reaction to it.
- **after** — Sam Altman publicly agrees with pacing the frontier, the position
  set out in Amodei's essay.
- **after** — Elon Musk publicly agrees with pacing the frontier, the position
  set out in Amodei's essay.
- **after** — David Sacks publicly attacks the proposal as cartel-formation and
  attacks METR's independence, responding to Amodei's essay.
- **after** — METR is named in Amodei's proposal as the evaluator that would hold
  the gate, without having sought that role. It issues no public act.
- **after** — Donald Trump argues publicly for acceleration and against Amodei's
  pacing position, bringing Jensen Huang on stage.
- **after** — Jensen Huang argues on stage for acceleration, against the pacing
  position set out in Amodei's essay.
- **after** — A DeepSeek engineer publicly attacks Anthropic in extreme terms,
  responding to the resignation post.
- **after** — Conspiracy readings circulate claiming the proposal is a
  regulatory-capture play and that the other lab leaders' agreement is
  insincere. These are claims about the reactions, not about the resignation.
- **2026-09-19** — A commentary video ("Slow Down or Die", The PrimeTime)
  summarises the resignation, the essay, the reactions to the essay and the
  conspiracy readings, and layers its own reading on top of them.
