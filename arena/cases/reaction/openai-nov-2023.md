# OpenAI board removes Sam Altman
as of: 2023-11-17

## Chronology — dated, sourced facts only

- **2023-11-17** — The OpenAI board publishes a statement removing Sam Altman as
  CEO, saying he "was not consistently candid in his communications with the
  board." Mira Murati is named interim CEO.
- **2023-11-17, shortly after** — Greg Brockman posts that he is quitting, citing
  the board's decision on Altman.
- **2023-11-17, evening** — Altman posts publicly about his removal.
- **2023-11-17, evening** — Several senior researchers resign, naming the board's
  removal of Altman as their reason.
- **2023-11-19** — Satya Nadella announces Microsoft is hiring Altman and
  Brockman to lead a new research group, responding to their departure from
  OpenAI.
- **2023-11-20** — Roughly 700 of ~770 OpenAI employees sign an open letter
  threatening to resign and join Microsoft's new group unless the board resigns.
  The letter cites the Microsoft offer as an available destination and demands
  reversal of the board's action.
- **2023-11-20** — Ilya Sutskever, a board member who had voted to remove Altman,
  signs the employee letter and posts publicly that he regrets his participation
  in the board's actions.
- **2023-11-21** — Altman returns as CEO under a reconstituted board.
- **after** — Press analysis of the employee letter as a demonstration of where
  power actually sat, and commentary arguing the episode showed the nonprofit
  board structure was unenforceable.
